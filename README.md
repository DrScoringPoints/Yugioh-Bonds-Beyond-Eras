# Yu-Gi-Oh! Bonds Beyond Eras (BBE)

A fan-made, personal-use Yu-Gi-Oh! game for Windows and Android, built on the open-source EDOPro engine. It's inspired by Tag Force 3, with every anime era from Duel Monsters to VRAINS. Not affiliated with Konami, and not for sale.

## Current features
- **Story Mode**: student life at Meridian University. Pick a Tag Force-style partner from any era, explore a 3D overworld, take on duelists from every era, and work toward graduation.
- **Degree**: Pick your degree in story mode, and earn perks in the story, your partners perk will also count.
- **Partner Loyalty:**: Keep your partner happy by winning duels, and passing class, or they may find someone else to tag with!
- **Free Duel**: duel any of 250 era-accurate duelists with your own deck, a borrowed deck or a rental deck. Tag up with any partner from any era, including 68 Original Characters.
- **Tournaments**: preset cups plus a new bracket mode with Singles (up to 32), Tag (up to 64) and CPU vs CPU. Watch or skip each match.
- **Toon Corner Card Shop**: real booster sets priced by age, daily singles, the Creator's Private Collection (15,000 DP) and buy-once Starter and Structure Decks.
- **Trunk and Deck Editor**: collect cards, build 40-card decks and use duelist deck recipes. Follows the current TCG rules, with an optional Forbidden & Limited list.
- **Minigames**: DP-earning minigames such as Blackjack, darts, Card Toss Range, Attribute Sort, and Spirit Shell
- **Duel features**: a pause that truly freezes the duel, Restart Duel, and controller, keyboard, mouse and touch (Android) support.
- **Updates**: an in-game update check, plus a custom installer and updater.

## Planned
- The full English Starter and Structure Deck lineup (81 decks), shown as retail boxes on the Toon Corner walls.
- Fully functional story mode with unique story for 49 characters, including 2 special story modes with final boss and secret boss. the remaining 151 will have a generic story that depends on their personality.
- Overworld map with 9 pockets, and 4-6 zones per pocket. you will walk around like Tag Force 3 and challenge to single or tag duel. Go to class and participate in minigame, or go to underground duels for extra DP.
- Active card lists always being maintained for English releases
- Effect activation banners that explain why a card activated.
- Smarter AI (better trap timing, removal targets and lock awareness).
- A card art audit, so every card shows its English TCG art.
- A minigame overhaul with new modes and harder difficulties.
- More variety in deck recipes.
- A Tag Force 3-style heart and partner system.
- Knockout Tag: a 2-vs-2 mode with 4 separate fields and 8,000 LP each. Knock out one opponent and it becomes 2-on-1. Defend your partner with your own monsters, run up to 4 Field Spells at once, and pick the turn order and Extra Monster Zone layout. Also coming to Tournament mode.
- Era Formats: 5 rulesets (DM+GX, 5D's, ZEXAL, ARC-V and Modern) that only allow cards from that era, judged by each card's first release date, so no Synchros in a DM+GX duel. Every duelist gets a hidden era deck built in the spirit of their time, with classic staples like Pot of Greed back in play. Era rules also come to tournaments, and the Trunk gets an Era filter.
